## Context used for the prompts

The prompts were informed by multiple independent sources rather than a single page:

- Faiz E Aam’s public listing mentions Hifz-ul-Quraan, modern education, transport, hostel admission, picnic activity, and a boys wing: <https://www.schoolandcollegelistings.com/IN/Bandipora/307263003428146/FAIZ-E-AAM-SECONDARY-SCHOOL-BANDIPORA>
- The District Bandipora education page describes classrooms, libraries, labs, creativity, teamwork, problem-solving, and extracurricular development: <https://bandipore.nic.in/education/>
- A Rising Kashmir report describes Faiz-e-Aam students taking part in a role-based educational visit: <https://risingkashmir.com/bandipora-police-hosts-students-from-faiz-e-aam-secondary-school-for-immersive-policing-experience/>
- A KNS report describes Bandipora students visiting a biotechnology department to connect classroom learning with practical laboratory work: <https://www.knskashmir.com/bandipora-school-students-visit-kashmir-university-biotechnology-department-205214>

No source student image was copied. Do not add real pupil names, school logos, readable generated signage, or claim these are photographs from a specific school event.
